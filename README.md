# Title

Modeling the Relationship Between Study Time and Academic Performance Using Mathematical and Computational Methods

## Research Question

Can mathematical models accurately describe the relationship between study time and academic performance, and is there evidence of diminishing returns?

# Why
Students often assume that studying longer always leads to better grades. However, the relationship between study time and academic performance may not be linear. Understanding this relationship can help students allocate their time more effectively while providing educators with insights into learning behavior. This project investigates whether mathematical models can describe this relationship and whether additional study hours eventually produce diminishing academic returns.

# Objectives

- Collect data on study habits and academic performance.
- Develop several mathematical models.
- Compare which model best describes the data.
- Use calculus to interpret the rate of change in academic performance.
- Determine whether diminishing returns exist.
