### 2. Introduction
A commom misconception among students is that academic success is directly proportional to time spent studying: the longer one spends studying, the higher their final score will be. However, human cognitive capacity, after school commitment, sleep schedule and fatigue suggest that this  relationship may not always be linear.

While dedicated practice is essential for mastery, extended hours of studying can and would lead to exhaustion, reduced focus, and ultimately a break in performance.
