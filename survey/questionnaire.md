# Survey Questions
Purpose: Educational Research Project

Thank you for taking time to complete this survey. The purpose of this survey is to gather data on student study habits, lifestyle patterns, and academic performance for an educational research assignments.

Before you begin, please review the following information: 
- Voluntary Participation: Participating in this survey is completely voluntary. You are free to to skip any question you do not feel comfortable answering.
- Right to Withdraw: You may exit or stop participating in the survey at any time without penalty or consequences.
- Anonymity & Privacy: Your responses are completely anonymous. No names, email addresses, IP addresses, or identifying personal information will be collected or stored.
- Use of Data: The information collected will be used strictly for class research and educational analysis.

## Demographics
-  How old are you ?
-  What's your current year in school ?
-  What is your academic major or primary field of study ?

## Academic Information
-  What is your current cumulative GPA ?
-  How many credit hours are you taking this semester ?

## Study Habits
-  On average, how many hours do you spend studying per week outside of class ?
-  What study method you use most frequently ?
    - Studying alone 
        - With music
        - In a quiet space (library)
    - Studying with a peer group
    - Tutoring sessions / Academic Center
- On average, how many total hours do you study for a major exam in the days leading up to it ?

## LifeStyle Variables
- On average, how many hours of sleep do you get per night during the academic term ?
- How many hours do you work at a paid job per week ?
- On an average, what percentage of your scheduled classes do you attend ?
- Do you regularly participate in off campus activities (eg. clubs, sports, volunteering) ?
